 - Still improving at the end of the budget: 6 of 12 runs (Pre-LN + Full softmax, Post-LN + Sampled softmax, LiGR + BCE, LiGR + Full softmax, Pre-LN + BCE, LiGR + Sampled softmax) - more time would likely raise their scores

====================================================================================================
 7. READ BEFORE QUOTING THESE NUMBERS
====================================================================================================
 - One seed per combination: no error bars. Differences of a few thousandths of NDCG may be noise.
 - Equal time budget, not equal epochs: slower combinations get fewer epochs. That is the
   intended consumer-hardware question, but it is not the paper's protocol.
 - LiGR and gBCE are implemented from the papers' descriptions, not from official code.
   gBCE collapses here with t=0.75 and t=0 and with 32 or 256 negatives, while sampled
   softmax works using the same negatives - so the formula and scoring are not the cause.
   The likely cause is summed-negative BCE dynamics under a short budget. Unresolved.
 - Latency = median of 7 passes of 100 single requests, with the process pinned to
   performance cores. Unpinned, the same model measured ~1.6 ms or ~4.5 ms depending on
   which core type Windows chose. Latency on another machine depends on its CPU too.
 - Not comparable to the eSASRec paper's numbers: different split, model size and training length.
